# Power BI Dashboard

## Overview

This folder contains the Power BI dashboard file for the E-commerce Business Performance Analysis project.

The dashboard was developed using Power BI, with a focus on interactive business reporting, data modeling, and DAX-based metrics.

## Dashboard File

- `e-commerce-business-performance-analysis.pbix` — Power BI report containing the interactive dashboard and data model.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary

Provides an overview of business performance through key performance indicators, revenue trends, revenue by sales channel, and sales by payment method.

![Executive Summary](../images/dashboard_page1.png)

### 2. Sales & Product Performance

Analyzes product and sales performance through revenue and quantity by category, top 10 products by revenue, and revenue by brand.

![Sales & Product Performance](../images/dashboard_page2.png)

### 3. Customer & Marketing Analysis

Presents customer metrics and marketing funnel performance, including new customers in 2024, purchasing customers in 2024, funnel events by status, new customers by registration channel, and conversion rate by channel source.

![Customer & Marketing Analysis](../images/dashboard_page3.png)
